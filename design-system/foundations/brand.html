eclume

eco + lume: five bars of a spoken word, cut square. The mark is always walnut on linen, or linen on walnut and ink. Always write the wordmark as eclume, lowercase and Archivo 800. Clear space is one bar height (the mark's width) on every side. The app icon is a solid walnut square with the linen mark at 72%. Never round it, tint it or outline it.
