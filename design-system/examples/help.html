Common questions

Why is my recording still processing?

Recordings wait in a queue for the GPU worker. The pipeline on the recording page names the step it is on. A one-hour meeting usually finishes in a few minutes once it reaches the front of the queue.

Who can see my recordings?

You, and the people you share a collection with. Administrators manage the server but see recordings only when you share them.

Can I delete a recording for everyone?

The owner of a recording can delete it. Deleting removes the audio, the transcript and the summary.

Which file types can I import?

Most audio and video files. The browser converts them before upload, so large videos upload as audio only.

How voice suggestions work

4 min readUpdated 25 Sep 2026

When a transcript has a speaker with no person linked, eclume can suggest who it is by comparing the voice with the voice prints of people in your organization.

What a voice print is

A voice print is a set of samples from one person: the stretches of speech where someone confirmed that a speaker was that person. It uses the same model that separates speakers in the transcript.

When eclume suggests a person

A suggestion appears when the voice is close enough to a voice print to pass your organization's threshold.

  • A suggestion never links on its own. It becomes a link only when someone accepts it.
  • Accepting adds the speaker's samples to that person's voice print, so later suggestions get better.
  • Rejecting keeps the speaker unlinked and does not change the voice print.
Off by defaultYour organization has to turn voice prints on in the admin panel before any suggestion appears.

Consent and removal

A voice print is sensitive biometric data under the LGPD. eclume keeps one only when the organization has opted in and the person has consented. A person can withdraw consent at any time; their voice print is deleted and existing links stay as they are.
