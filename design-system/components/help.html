FAQ
Why is my recording still processing?

Recordings wait in a queue for the GPU worker. The pipeline on the recording page shows the step it is on. A one-hour meeting usually takes a few minutes.

Can I delete a recording for everyone?

Only the owner and administrators can delete a recording.

Which file types can I import?

Most audio and video files.
