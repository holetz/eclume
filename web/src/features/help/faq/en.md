## Why is my recording still processing?

Recordings wait in a queue for the server's transcription processor. The step bar on the recording page shows which step it is on. A one-hour meeting is usually ready a few minutes after its turn comes.

## Who sees my recordings?

Inside eclume, only you: each account has its own space, and there is no sharing between accounts. Whoever administers the server takes care of the configuration and health of the service, and the administration panel shows no titles, transcripts or summaries. Outside the server, the transcript text may go to the AI agent that writes the summaries, fields and chat, if whoever administers chose an external service. See where each piece of data goes on this server in **My settings › Account › Privacy and data**.

## Can I delete a recording?

Yes. On the recording page, use **Delete**. This erases the audio, the transcript, the summary and the fields, and cannot be undone.

## Which files can I import?

Most audio and video files. The browser converts the file before sending, so a large video goes up as audio only. The recording date is read from the file, and you confirm it before sending.

## Do I need to ask the people I record for their consent?

Yes. When creating the account, you accepted responsibility for that. eclume does not record each person's consent: agree with them before recording.

## Can an AI assistant delete my recordings?

Only if its token has the **Delete recordings** scope, which comes unticked. Without it, the assistant gets a refusal and explains what is missing.
