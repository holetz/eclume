A field's rule, in **How to identify**, is what the agent reads to fill in that field from the
transcript. Write it as you would explain to someone who will listen to the meeting for you.

## What a good rule says

1. **What to look for:** which information from the conversation goes in the field.
2. **The shape of the value:** short or full name, capitals, the format of a date.
3. **When to leave it empty:** what to do when the conversation does not carry the information.

## Example: the Client field

> **How to identify**
> The customer is the company the discussed work is done for. Use the short name: "Guion", not
> "Guion Engenharia Ltda". Leave it empty when no customer is mentioned.

## Tips

- One rule per field. For two pieces of information, create two fields.
- In choice-type fields, the agent only uses the options you defined. You do not need to repeat them
  in the rule.
- The title has no rule here: each agenda type says how to write the subject and builds the title
  with it. See [Build the title from the agenda type](/ajuda/title-pattern).

Before saving, test the rule: [Test a rule before saving](/ajuda/test-a-rule).
