The simplest way to record a meeting is to leave the eclume desktop app open on the computer where
you join it. It takes care of recording and uploading, and you only go back to the library to read
the summary.

## First steps

While the account is new, the library shows the **First steps** card, with three steps:

1. **Bring in the first recording:** import a file, record with the desktop app or
   [on your phone](/ajuda/record-on-your-phone).
2. **Check the agenda types:** they give the title and the summary format of each meeting. See
   [Configure an agenda type](/ajuda/configure-agenda-type).
3. **Link a speaker to a person:** in the recording's Speakers block, say who each speaker is. See
   [Link a speaker to a person](/ajuda/link-speaker-to-person).

Each step is ticked when done, and the card disappears on its own with all three done. **Dismiss**
hides it in this browser; **Restore the defaults**, in Environment, brings it back.

## Before you start

Install the app and sign in with your email and password: see
[Install the desktop app](/ajuda/install-desktop-app). Without the app at hand, you can also
[import a file](/ajuda/import-files) you already recorded.

> **Agree with whoever is recorded**
> When you created your account, you accepted responsibility for asking the people you record for
> their consent. Say so at the start of the meeting and record only those who agree.

## During the meeting

- The app notices when a meeting starts in Teams, Meet or Zoom. Depending on what you chose for each
  app, it records on its own or asks first.
- It records your microphone and the computer's audio, so both sides of the conversation come in.
- To remember an important moment, use the **Marker** button or the shortcut. See
  [Markers made during the recording](/ajuda/markers).

## After the meeting

When the meeting ends, the app uploads the recording on its own, even with the window closed. It shows
up in the [library](/) and goes through the processing steps: transcription, filling the fields,
picking the agenda type and summary. See
[What each processing step does](/ajuda/processing-stages).
