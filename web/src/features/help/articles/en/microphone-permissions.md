To record, the app needs the microphone. If the recording comes out silent or the app warns that it
did not find the microphone, check the permission in the system.

## Windows

1. Open **Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone**.
2. Turn on **Microphone access** and **Let desktop apps access your microphone**.
3. Close and open eclume again.

If the computer has more than one microphone, choose the right one in **Settings → System → Sound →
Input**.

## Linux

Linux does not ask permission for the microphone: the app uses what the sound system points to as
input. If the recording comes out silent, open the sound settings and check the chosen **Input** and
its volume. The meeting's sound (what the others say) comes from the system's default output.

## Android

On the first recording, the phone asks whether eclume may use the microphone: tap **Allow**.
If you denied it before:

1. Open **Settings → Apps → eclume → Permissions**.
2. Under **Microphone**, choose **Allow only while using the app**.
3. Go back to eclume and record again.

Also allow **notifications**: they are how the phone shows it is recording with the screen locked.
