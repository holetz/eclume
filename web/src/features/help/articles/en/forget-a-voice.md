A person's voiceprint is the set of samples eclume uses to suggest who is speaking in a recording.
You can delete it at any time.

## How to delete it

1. Open the person in [People](/pessoas).
2. Go to the **Voice samples** tab.
3. Use **Forget the voice** and confirm.

## What goes and what stays

- **Goes:** all the voiceprint samples and the pending suggestions with that person.
- **Stays:** the speaker links you already confirmed, the transcripts and the utterances.

If you link a speaker to that person again, a new sample comes in, and suggestions may return.

## When to do it

Do it when the person asks. A voiceprint is biometric data under the LGPD, and agreeing with the
person about keeping it is your responsibility, as the notice you accepted when creating the account says.
