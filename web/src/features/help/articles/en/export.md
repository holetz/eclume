On the recording page, the **Export** menu downloads the transcript or the summary as a file.

## The formats

- **TXT:** the transcript as running text, with the name of whoever speaks. Good for pasting anywhere.
- **SRT:** the transcript with the timing of each utterance, in subtitle format. Useful for adding
  subtitles to the meeting video.
- **MD:** the summary in Markdown, with headings and lists. It pastes well into note tools and GitHub.
- **DOCX:** the transcript and the summary in a Word document, to send or print.

## What goes in

The export uses the current version: your corrections to the utterances, the names of the people
linked to the speakers and the most recent version of the summary.
