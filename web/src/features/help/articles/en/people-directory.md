Besides the name, a person can have **company**, **department**, **role**, **email**, **phone**,
**person tags** and notes. All of it is optional. With this data, the library answers questions like
"in which meetings was someone from company X".

## Fill in the record

1. In [People](/pessoas), register someone in the first row of the table: name and, if you want, the
   company. Enter also adds.
2. Open the person and use **Edit** for the other data. Company and department suggest the values you
   already used, so the same company does not become two.
3. **Person tags** are free labels, like "customer", "board" or "supplier". They are separate from
   the recordings' tags.

## The name helps the transcription

The names of the people you register go in as vocabulary in your account's following transcripts, so
recognition gets right the proper names it usually misses. It applies to every new transcription,
including when transcribing again. It is a help, not a guarantee. Up to 50 names go in, within a
limit of 400 characters, in the record's alphabetical order; whoever is past the limit is left out.
Only your account's names are used.

## Choose the columns

The **Columns** button, beside the search, shows or hides company, department, role, tags, email,
phone, recordings, voice samples and the last recording. Email starts hidden. The choice is saved in
this browser.

## Filter the library by participants

In the library's filter panel, the **Participants** group lists the companies, departments and person
tags in use. Ticking values shows the recordings in which someone spoke who has **all** you ticked:
company "Agro Sul" with the tag "board" brings the meetings with someone from Agro Sul's board, not
the meetings where one person is from Agro Sul and another, from elsewhere, is on the board. Two
values of the same item count as "one or the other".

The filter sees only who is linked to a speaker of the recording. See
[Link a speaker to a person](/ajuda/link-speaker-to-person).
