The phone app is a portable recorder. It is for conversations away from the computer: a visit, a
hallway, an in-person meeting.

## What the phone does

- Records offline and with the screen locked.
- Marks important instants, with a short note if you like.
- Keeps the recordings on the device while you are away.
- Syncs on its own over Wi-Fi when it returns to this server's network. Over mobile data, nothing leaves.

The phone does not show transcripts or summaries. To read them, use the web.

## How to connect

1. Install the app on the phone.
2. On the app's first screen, tap **Read a code**.
3. Point the camera at the QR on the [Mobile app](/app-movel) page.

No camera? The same page shows the code as text for you to type in the app. On the server's network,
the app also finds the server on its own and signs in with your email and password.
