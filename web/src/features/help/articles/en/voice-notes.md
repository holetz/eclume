A voice note is a recording of one person alone: an idea, a reminder, a dictated draft.

## How to record

- **In the desktop app:** use **New note**, the tray item or the shortcut (`Ctrl+Alt+N` on Windows
  and Linux, `⌘⇧N` on macOS). On Linux with Wayland (recent Ubuntu), the shortcut works after
  **Create GNOME shortcuts**, in Settings · Audio. The note records the microphone only and stops
  on its own after a few seconds of silence.
- **On the phone:** record as usual. The note syncs over Wi-Fi, like the other recordings.

## What happens to it

The note goes through the same steps as a meeting, but it is transcribed with a single speaker, with
no voice separation, and lands in the agenda type **Nota de voz** (voice note). That type's summary is
your own text, cleaned of hesitations and split into paragraphs, ready to copy.

In the library, notes have a mark of their own, and the **Type** filter separates notes from meetings.

If the note has more than one voice, use **Transcribe again in…** on the recording and fill in the
minimum and maximum number of speakers.
