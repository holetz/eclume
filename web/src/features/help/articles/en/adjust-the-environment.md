The **Environment** screen keeps how eclume looks and behaves in this browser. Open it from the gear
beside your name, in the header, and choose **Environment** in the menu.

These settings stay in this browser only: they do not change your account, and another computer or
your phone keep their own.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: light, dark or the same as the system.
- **Density**: compact tightens the spaces between blocks to fit more on screen. The text size does
  not change.
- **Motion**: reducing it turns off animations and transitions, even if the system does not ask for it.

## Agenda

- **Working hours**: the hours highlighted in the library's week. The week opens showing the whole
  working day; the rest of the day is still there, by scrolling the grid or zooming out.
- **Open the library in**: the last view you used (with its filters) or always the week, the month
  or the list.

## Zoom in the week

In the library's week, the magnifier buttons in the corner over the hours zoom in and out. With the
mouse over the grid, **Ctrl + wheel** does the same and keeps the hour under the cursor in place. The
minimum zoom shows the whole day on screen.

## Restore the defaults

The button at the end of the screen takes everything back to the start: system theme, comfortable
density, system motion, working hours from 08:00 to 18:00 and the library on the last view used.
