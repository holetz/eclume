The automatic transcript gets proper names, acronyms and noisy passages wrong. You fix everything on
the recording page.

## Edit an utterance

Open the transcript, choose the utterance and edit the text. The correction counts for search and
for exports. The utterance's timing does not change.

## Rename and join speakers

Open the **Speakers** block, right above the transcript; collapsed, it already shows who speaks.

- **Rename:** give the speaker a name when it is not someone you register as a person.
- **Join:** when the transcript split the same person into two speakers (S2 and S4, for example),
  join the two. The utterances move to one.

Joining fixes too many speakers, but does not split voices the transcript mixed into one. When the
number of speakers came out wrong, instead of joining one by one, use **Transcribe again in…** and
fill in the **Minimum speakers** and **Maximum speakers**: the new transcript separates the voices
within those limits. Left empty, the platform's limits apply.

To name whoever really speaks, prefer
[linking the speaker to a person](/ajuda/link-speaker-to-person).

## Transcribe again

Transcribing again makes a new transcript of the audio and redoes the following steps. In
**Transcribe again in…** you choose the language and the minimum and maximum number of speakers. Text
corrections to the previous transcript do not carry over to the new one, so correct after
transcribing again. The speaker-to-person links you confirmed still hold. See [Try a failed step again](/ajuda/try-again).
