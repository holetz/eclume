[Search](/busca) looks through the text of all your transcripts and shows the passages where the
words appear.

## How to search

Type the words. The result comes grouped by recording, with the total at the top
("7 passages in 3 recordings") and the words highlighted in each passage.

## Filters

Filters refine the search and combine:

- **Period:** from one date to another.
- **Person:** only the utterances of whoever you linked to a speaker.
- **Tag** and **Collection.**
- **Your fields**, such as "Client".

In the [library](/), the filter panel on the right has the same filters and two more: **Type**
(meetings or notes) and **Status** (ready, processing or failed).

## Ask the Agent instead of searching

The **Agent** item, the first in the menu, opens the [Agent](/agente) screen: there you ask a
question in plain language about your set of recordings. The agent starts from the summaries and
only reads the transcripts when the summary is not enough; each answer shows the recordings it relies
on, with the link to open them. See [Chat with the Agent](/ajuda/chat-with-the-agent).

## From the passage to the audio

Click the passage: the recording opens at the exact second of the utterance, with the player ready to play.
