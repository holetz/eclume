eclume transcribes each recording in one language. What decides it is, in this order: the language
chosen for the file, your account's default and the server's default.

## Your account's default

In **My settings › Account**, **Transcription language** says in which language your recordings are
transcribed when the file asks for none. The list always shows the real language of the default:
**Server default: Portuguese** means that what the administrator configured applies, today
Portuguese. The change applies to the next recordings; the ones already in the queue do not change.

## One language for one file

- **Web:** in the **Import files** window, each file has its own **Language** column, which starts
  from the account default.
- **Phone:** the **Recording saved** sheet, after you stop, lets you choose that recording's language.
- **Desktop:** the **Audio** tab of the settings chooses the language of this computer's meetings and
  notes; **Account default** lets your account's apply.

## Detect automatically

**Detect automatically** asks the transcription engine to find out the language. It is for people who
record in different languages. After transcription, the recording shows the detected language.

## Transcribe again in another language

If a recording came out in the wrong language, open the recording and use **Transcribe again in…**.
Choose the language and confirm. The transcript is redone in a new revision: text corrections made by
hand are lost, the people linked to speakers come back, and the fields, agenda type and summary are
redone. While a transcription is in progress, you cannot ask for another.
