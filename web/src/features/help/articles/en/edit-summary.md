The summary is yours: you can fix a name, delete an item or rewrite a whole passage.

## How to edit

1. On the recording page, use **Edit** in the summary bar.
2. Change the text. The summary is Markdown: `##` makes a heading and `-` makes a list item.
3. Use **Save**.

The edit becomes a **new version** of the summary, marked as manual. The version generated by the
agent stays saved: see [Summary versions and Back to the generated one](/ajuda/summary-versions).

## If another version came in while you were editing

While you edit, the summary may change another way: an adjustment through the chat, a new summary
after changing the agenda type or an assistant connected through MCP. In that case, eclume warns you
and overwrites nothing. Copy your text, open the new version and apply your change again.

## The earlier-version summary warning

If you change the transcript after the summary was written, the summary bar shows the warning
**Summary of an earlier version of the transcript**. Correcting an utterance, renaming, linking or
joining speakers counts as a change. The warning says the summary text does not yet reflect that
change; it does not erase or alter anything. Use **Regenerate** for the agent to write a new version
from the transcript as it is now. If your text is already right, you can go on without regenerating.

## Prefer asking over writing?

For bigger changes, such as "put the decisions in a table", ask the agent:
[Adjust the summary by chat](/ajuda/adjust-by-chat).
