The transcript splits the conversation by speaker (S1, S2, S3…), but does not know who each one is.
Linking a speaker to a person names the utterances.

## On the recording page

1. Open the **Speakers** block, right above the transcript: each speaker has a card.
2. On each card, use **Link to a person** and choose the person from the list. Start typing the name to filter.
3. The person is not registered yet? Use **Register** right in the list: the name is enough.

The link takes effect right away, on all of that speaker's utterances.

## What the link changes

- The utterances show the person's name instead of S1, S2.
- The person's page, in [People](/pessoas), lists their recordings and utterances.
- Search filters by person: see [Search your recordings](/ajuda/search-recordings).
- With voice identification on at the server, the confirmed link becomes a sample of the person's
  voice, and the next recordings may suggest who it is. See
  [How voice suggestions work](/ajuda/voice-suggestions).
- With automatic assignment, eclume can also link the speaker on its own when the voice is very
  similar; the speaker shows as **Assigned by voice** and you confirm or switch it off.

## Picked the wrong person?

Choose another person for the same speaker, or undo the link. The utterances go back to showing the
transcript's speaker.
